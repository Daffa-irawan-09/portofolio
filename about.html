About Me

I'm Daffa Irawan, a Software Engineering student based in Jakarta.

I study Rekayasa Perangkat Lunak (Software Engineering) at SMKN 6 Jakarta, where I learn the fundamentals of building software properly — from planning and databases to interfaces and deployment.

Outside the classroom, I'm part of Index Studio, where I get to apply what I learn on real projects alongside other developers. I enjoy working across different kinds of software: web platforms, desktop tools, and the occasional game prototype in Godot.

I care about writing code that's easy to maintain and interfaces that are easy to use — and I'm always looking for the next thing to build.
